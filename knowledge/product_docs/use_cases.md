# Tech Profuse - Use Cases and Success Stories

## Artificial Intelligence Use Cases

### Lead Qualification

Organizations with high inbound inquiry volume can use Tech Profuse Agentic AI
to qualify leads automatically. AI agents assess inquiries, identify buying
signals, and route high-intent opportunities to human sales teams.

Best fit:

- B2B sales teams
- Service businesses
- SaaS companies
- Companies with repetitive inbound qualification work

Outcome:

- Faster qualification
- Better sales focus
- 24/7 lead handling
- Reduced manual triage

### Voice Call Analytics

Tech Profuse AI systems can transcribe and analyze calls for intent, sentiment,

Best fit:

Outcome:

- Better insight into customer conversations
- Faster quality review
- Compliance monitoring
- Identification of recurring customer concerns

## Cybersecurity and Remote Access Use Cases

### Endpoint Protection

Organizations can deploy Kaspersky Cloud Pro or Cloud Plus through Tech Profuse
to protect workstations, servers, and mobile devices from malware, ransomware,
and phishing.

Outcome:

- Centralized cloud-based endpoint management
- Threat detection using behavioral analysis, machine learning, and threat
  intelligence
- Installation, configuration, monitoring, and support from Tech Profuse

### Identity, Access, Compliance, and DLP

Organizations can use Plurilock AI / Cloud Codes to apply layered access
controls, SSO, MFA, compliance scanning, and data loss prevention.

Outcome:

- Stronger identity controls
- IP, browser, location, and time-based access restriction
- Passwordless login options through FIDO2
- Google Drive sharing controls
- Email compliance rules
- Clipboard, screenshot, Gmail, and external sharing restrictions at the DLP
  tier

### Secure Remote Desktop

Organizations can buy, configure, and deploy AnyDesk through Tech Profuse as an
authorized AnyDesk partner in India.

Outcome:

- Localized pricing
- Correct licensing guidance
- Remote desktop setup and training
- Secure remote access with TLS 1.2, RSA 2048, permissions, and 2FA guidance
